# Prototype Limitations & Scope Disclosures
## Mandatory Engineering Integrity Statement

1. **Simulation-Only Operating Mode:**  
   The prototype runs in a sandboxed supervisory simulation environment. It does **not** directly actuate field Variable Frequency Drives, steam generators, or surface wellheads. All setpoint dispatches are simulated within the digital twin.
2. **Synthetic Data Disclosure:**  
   The 15 Baghewala wells (`BGW-001` through `BGW-015`) and demonstration well `BGW-017` are initialized using calibrated synthetic/configured data derived from public literature and SIH problem statements. Actual field deployment requires calibration against Oil India Limited live SCADA feeds.
3. **Model Prediction Horizon:**  
   The remaining useful life (RUL) numbers and failure probabilities represent model-estimated hazard horizons based on historical failure modes (rod parting, tubing leaks, plunger wear). They must not be interpreted as physical guarantees.
4. **Safety & Operator Authority:**  
   All optimizations generated by THERMOLIFT X require explicit human operator review and approval. The Safety Governor acts as an envelope boundary, but human petroleum engineers maintain final operational responsibility.
